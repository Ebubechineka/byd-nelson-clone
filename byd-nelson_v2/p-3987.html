Ute Canopies for Your BYD Shark 6

Looking for a canopy for your BYD Shark 6? BYD Nelson offers a range of quality ute canopies to suit different styles, budgets and practical requirements. Below you will see the brands we stock, if you are after something not listed, contact our team as we can source all brands. Our workshop in Nelson can also fit these canopies to any model of ute, not just the Shark 6!

Our canopy range includes the M2 Overland Artemis, Ute Master Centurion, ARB UV-stable ABS thermoplastic canopies, Fully Equipped Defender, Crown and Fleetline options. Each canopy has its own design, features and customisation options, so choosing the right one often comes down to the look and layout you prefer.

The M2 Overland Artemis and Ute Master Centurion are premium canopy options, offering a sophisticated, highly integrated design with a range of optional extras available. The Fully Equipped Defender provides a more affordable alternative while still delivering a stylish, practical canopy setup.

For those looking for the durability and versatility of thermoplastic construction, we also offer ARB UV-stable ABS thermoplastic, Crown and Fleetline canopies. These options provide different designs and configurations, allowing you to tailor your BYD Shark 6 to suit your needs.

Many of our canopy options can be customised with accessories and premium upgrades, including storage solutions and other practical additions. Available options and pricing vary between canopy ranges.

There is no single “best” canopy — the right choice depends on your preferred design, features, budget and how you plan to use your BYD Shark 6. 

Build your own, or choose from pre-configured options

Our certified BYD service agents, The Car Company Automotive, are experts at fitting all makes and models of canopies. They can also fit canopies for Hilux, Ranger, Triton, Navara, Amarok, D-Max and more. Not sure which canopy is right for your needs? Enquire with our experts!
