Benefits of leasing for businesses

One monthly payment & no cash deposit

No cash upfront is needed to lease a brand new vehicle. Just one fixed monthly payment can include scheduled servicing, tyres, discounted fuel, annual registration and maintenance costs.

Operating leases are tax deductible

Vehicle leasing and operating leases can be 100% tax deductible when treated as a business expense. 

Save time and hassle managing your vehicles

Our lease partners will manage your vehicle admin for you. They will send automated reminders of when your service or WoF is due, including posting your annual registration and RUC labels.

With a Fully Maintained Operating lease, just choose the term you want and kms you drive per year

One monthly payment includes a brand new vehicle plus:

  • All scheduled servicing & maintenance repairs
  • Replacement tyres
  • Roadside assistance 24/7
  • Accident management
  • Registration management
  • WoFs/CoFs
  • Relief vehicle service
  • Discounted fuel up to 13c/litre (or EV public charging fob)
Vehicle signage can be incorporated into your monthly payment

The cost of vehicle signage can be incorporated into business lease payments, allowing you to spread the expense over the lease term. This is a common option where the lease provider includes the cost of the signs, such as wraps or lettering, and adds it to your regular monthly payment. Alternatively, you can pay for the signage upfront. 

Most brand new vehicle makes and models can be leased, including light and heavy commercial vehicles

This gives you and your business flexibility when choosing what vehicle is right for your requirements.

Who can lease?

Our lease partners can lease just one vehicle, or up to 1000+ vehicles to businesses, including:

  • A limited liability company
  • GST registered businesses, including sole traders
  • An NZBN holder including non-profits, incorporated societies, charities and clubs

Qualifying questions for business owners

  • Are you looking for a vehicle for personal or business use?
  • Are you GST registered?
  • How long have you been in business for?
  • What options are you considering to fund this vehicle?
  • Have you considered leasing?
  • What budget would you be looking to pay per month?

Questions to identify future opportinity size

  • How many business vehicles do you have?
  • Do you own or lease them?
  • How often do you upgrade or replace your business vehicles?
  • Do you require more vehicles over the next 6-12 months?
