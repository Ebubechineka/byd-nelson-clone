2026 BYD SEALION 8 DM-P Premium AWD Ex-Demo, Just 2,790kms!

FLAGSHIP 7-SEAT LUXURY AVAILABLE FOR IMMEDIATE DELIVERY!

Experience the very best of BYD's Super Hybrid technology with this stunning 2026 SEALION 8 DM-P Premium AWD.

Finished in elegant Aurora White and travelled just 2,790kms, this ex-demonstrator offers an outstanding opportunity to step into BYD's flagship seven-seat SUV with savings!

Powered by BYD's advanced DM-P plug-in hybrid technology, the SEALION 8 delivers an impressive 359kW of power and 675Nm of torque, with intelligent all-wheel drive and acceleration from 0100km/h in just 4.9 seconds.

Its 35.6kWh Blade Battery offers up to 152km of electric driving range (NEDC), while the petrol hybrid system provides the flexibility for longer journeys without relying solely on charging infrastructure.

Inside, the Premium model delivers a truly luxurious experience, with genuine leather upholstery, heated, ventilated and massaging seats, premium audio and an impressive suite of technology.

Key Features Include:

-359kW / 675Nm DM-P Super Hybrid AWD
-35.6kWh Blade Battery Up to 152km EV Range (NEDC)
-7-Seater Luxury SUV
-Genuine Leather Interior
-Heated, Ventilated & Massaging Seats First & Second Rows
-Premium 21-Speaker Sound System
-Panoramic Glass Sunroof
-15.6" Infotainment Display & Head-Up Display
-Wireless Apple CarPlay & Android Auto
-360° Surround View Camera
-Adaptive Cruise Control & Advanced Safety Technology
-Tri-Zone Climate Control
-2,000kg Braked Towing Capacity

Whether you're transporting the family in comfort, heading away for the weekend or simply wanting a premium SUV that combines performance with everyday efficiency, the SEALION 8 delivers on every front.
