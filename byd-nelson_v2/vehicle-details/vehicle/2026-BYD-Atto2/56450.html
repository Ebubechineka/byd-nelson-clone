2026 BYD ATTO 2 EV Premium Ex-Demo, Just 685kms!

PREMIUM ELECTRIC SUV AVAILABLE FOR IMMEDIATE DELIVERY!

Looking to make the switch to electric? This 2026 BYD ATTO 2 Premium EV offers the perfect combination of modern technology, premium comfort and everyday practicality.

Finished in stunning Harbour Grey and travelled just 685kms, this ex-demonstrator presents a fantastic opportunity to get behind the wheel of BYD's latest compact electric SUV without the wait.

Powered by BYD's advanced 51.13kWh Blade Battery, the ATTO 2 delivers up to 345km of electric driving range (WLTP), making it an excellent choice for daily commuting, school runs and weekend adventures. With 130kW of power and 290Nm of torque, it offers smooth, responsive performance and the benefit of no petrol costs.

The higher-spec Premium model adds an impressive level of comfort and technology, with heated and ventilated front seats, panoramic glass roof and a modern, spacious interior.

Key Features Include:

-51.13kWh BYD Blade Battery
-Up to 345km Electric Range (WLTP)
-130kW Power / 290Nm Torque
-Heated & Ventilated Front Seats
-Panoramic Glass Roof
-360° Surround View Camera & Parking Sensors
-12.8" Rotating Touchscreen
-Wireless Apple CarPlay & Android Auto
-Wireless Phone Charging
-Adaptive Cruise Control & Advanced Safety Systems
-Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) Technology
-17" Alloy Wheels
-Practical 5-Seater SUV

Whether you're purchasing your first EV, upgrading your daily driver or looking for a practical second vehicle, the ATTO 2 Premium offers an excellent balance of efficiency, comfort and technology.
