2026 BYD SEALION 6 Dynamic Extended Custom Black-Out Package!

ONE-OF-A-KIND STYLING EX-DEMO WITH JUST 1,980KMS!

Stand out from the crowd with this stunning 2026 BYD SEALION 6 Dynamic Extended Range, finished in Stone Grey and enhanced with a custom black-out styling package.

With just 1,980kms, this ex-demonstrator offers a fantastic opportunity to secure a near-new Super Hybrid SUV with a distinctive appearance that sets it apart from the standard model.

The professionally styled black-out package creates a bold, sporty look, perfectly complementing the Stone Grey exterior.

Custom Black-Out Package Includes:
-Black Wrapped Roof
-Blacked-Out Cantrails
-Black Grille Accents
-Black Window Mouldings
-Black Mirror Covers

SUPER HYBRID EFFICIENCY EXTENDED ELECTRIC RANGE!

Powered by BYD's advanced DM-i Super Hybrid technology, the SEALION 6 combines smooth electric driving with the flexibility of a petrol engine for longer journeys.

The Dynamic Extended offers up to 140km of electric driving range and an estimated combined range of up to 1,100km, making it an excellent choice for daily commuting, family duties and weekend adventures.

Key Features Include:

-1.5L Petrol Plug-in Super Hybrid
-Up to 140km Electric Range (NEDC)
-160kW Power / 300Nm Torque
-Spacious 5-Seater SUV
-15.6" Rotating Touchscreen
-Wireless Apple CarPlay & Android Auto
-360° Surround View Camera
-Adaptive Cruise Control
-Blind Spot Monitoring & Advanced Safety Technology
-Automatic Emergency Braking
-Wireless Phone Charging
-Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) Technology
-Keyless Entry & Push-Button Start

With its spacious interior, impressive electric driving capability and striking custom styling, this SEALION 6 delivers an excellent balance of practicality, efficiency and road presence.
