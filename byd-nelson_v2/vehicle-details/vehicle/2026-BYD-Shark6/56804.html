2026 BYD SHARK 6 Premium AWD Ex-Demo, Just 1,650kms!

SUPER HYBRID PERFORMANCE AVAILABLE FOR IMMEDIATE DELIVERY!

Looking for a ute that delivers serious performance, impressive technology and everyday practicality? This 2026 BYD SHARK 6 Premium AWD combines the capability of a double-cab ute with the comfort of a premium SUV.

Finished in stunning Great White and travelled just 1,650kms, this ex-demonstrator presents a fantastic opportunity to get behind the wheel of BYD's innovative Super Hybrid ute without the wait.

Powered by a 1.5L turbocharged petrol engine paired with dual electric motors, the SHARK 6 produces an impressive 321kW of power and 650Nm of torque. With intelligent all-wheel drive and acceleration from 0100km/h in just 5.7 seconds, it delivers effortless performance whether you're commuting, heading to the worksite or getting away for the weekend.

The 29.58kWh Blade Battery provides up to 100km of electric driving range (NEDC), allowing you to complete many everyday journeys on electric power alone, while the petrol engine provides flexibility for longer trips.

Key Features Include:

-321kW / 650Nm DMO Super Hybrid AWD
-1.5L Turbo Petrol Plug-in Hybrid
-Up to 100km Electric Range (NEDC)
-2,500kg Braked Towing Capacity
-Heated & Ventilated Electric Front Seats
-Premium Dynaudio Sound System
-15.6" Rotating Touchscreen
-Wireless Apple CarPlay & Android Auto
-360° Surround View Camera
-Adaptive Cruise Control & Advanced Safety Technology
-Head-Up Display
-Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) Power Supply
-Spacious 5-Seater Double Cab

Whether you're after a capable work ute, a comfortable family vehicle or something versatile enough for weekend adventures, the SHARK 6 offers an exciting alternative to traditional diesel utes.
