350KW, 700NM, DUAL MOTOR AWD, 80KM EV RANGE

The Shark 6 has already changed the ute market. Now it's back and better than ever with the all-new 2.0L Turbo Super Hybrid powertrain and a massive 3,500kg braked towing capacity.

Combining the capability expected from a modern dual-cab ute with the performance, refinement and efficiency of BYD's advanced Super Hybrid technology, the Shark 6 is unlike anything else on the market.

Producing an impressive 350kW and 700Nm of combined power, the Shark 6 rockets from 0-100km/h in just 5.5 seconds while still offering the practicality needed for work, towing and family life.

Whether you're towing the boat, caravan or trailer, heading off-road for the weekend or simply looking for a ute that's comfortable and economical to live with every day, the Shark 6 delivers.

Key features include:

-NEW 2.0L Turbo Super Hybrid Powertrain
-3,500kg Braked Towing Capacity
-Dual Motor AWD System
-350kW Combined Power
-700Nm Combined Torque
-80km WLTP Electric Range
-360 Camera
-Adaptive Cruise Control
-Wireless Apple CarPlay & Android Auto
-Heated & Ventilated Front Seats
-15.6" Rotating Touchscreen
-Dynaudio Premium Audio System
-Head-Up Display
-Wireless Phone Charging
-Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) Capability

Unlike traditional diesel utes, the Shark 6 offers smooth electric driving around town, instant torque when you need it and exceptional refinement on the open road.

With double wishbone suspension front and rear, impressive off-road capability and a 700mm wading depth, it's just as comfortable exploring the back country as it is parked outside the office.

If you've been waiting for a ute that can genuinely do it all, the new Shark 6 deserves a closer look.

This is an Ex-Demo model and is now available for purchase! Get in contact with the team at BYD Nelson to arrange a test drive or to find out more information about this BYD Shark 6 Performance.
