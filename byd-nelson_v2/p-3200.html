Super Hybrid DMO Platform

The DMO Super Hybrid Off-road Platform, a core technology of BYD SHARK 6, stands as a revolutionary innovation, seamlessly combining rugged off-road capability and safety features with SUV-like comfort, all while maintaining impressive energy efficiency. This technology innovatively integrates a non-load-bearing frame designed for hybrids, the world’s first longitudinal EHS (Electric Hybrid System) drive assembly, CTC (Cell to Chassis) technology.
