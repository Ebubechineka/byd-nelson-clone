Super Hybrid, Super Spacious.

The Seal 6 Touring offers outstanding practicality with up to to 670 litres of boot space which can be expanded to almost 1,535 litres. It easily accommodates strollers, full-size suitcases and camping gear, making it the perfect vehicle for any adventure. Unique to BYD, Super Hybrid with DM Technology delivers class-leading performance with a 0-100 km/h time of 8.1 seconds and exceptional efficiency.

Blade Battery

The Blade battery is a keystone development from BYD. Developed over several years, the Blade battery is a lithium-ion phosphate (LFP) battery that has been engineered in such a way that it has passed rigorous puncture and pressure tests. Due to it’s unique design the Blade Battery is stronger, longer lasting, stores more power and offers greater range while delivering a higher level of safety than conventional designs.

Hands-free power tailgate

The smart electric tailgate offers one-touch control, adjustable opening height, and smooth, quiet operation. Intelligent sensors provide reliable, hands-free access, making loading effortless in any situation.

Rugged roof rails

With the Touring, you’ll find sturdy roof rails to accommodate everything from mountain bikes to surfboards and rooftop cargo boxes, so you can travel anywhere with ease.

Clear views, whatever the weather

Designed with long-distance journeys in mind, the versatile, dedicated rear wiper ensures a clear rear view in all-weather conditions.
